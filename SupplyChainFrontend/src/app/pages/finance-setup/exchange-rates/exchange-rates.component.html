Exchange Rates

One unit of the first currency is worth the rate in the second, from the effective date until the next rate for the same pair. A document uses the latest rate on or before its date — or the opposite pair's, turned round — and keeps it once final, so changing a rate here never changes a document already issued or approved.

You can see the rates. Changing them needs the Manage Finance Setup permission.
