## Part B — contract deltas

- PO create/update/read: `orderDate` (yyyy-MM-dd). PO line read/write: `originalCurrencyId?, originalCurrencyPrice?, exchangeRateUsed?` (read-only/server-owned), `priceSource` may be 4 on read.
- `POST /api/purchase/resolve-price`: see E-9. Backward compatible.
- PR→PO conversion response: add `pricingSummary {totalLines, pricedCount, unpricedCount, warnings[]}`.
- `POST /api/finance/convert-currency` (verify existing shape first; extend additively): `{amount, fromCurrencyId|fromCurrencyCode, toCurrencyId|toCurrencyCode, effectiveDate}` → `{success, convertedAmount, rateUsed, rateDate, fromCurrencyCode, toCurrencyCode, errorMessage}`; no-rate = 200 + `success:false`.
